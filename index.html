Homemade Gelato

A Traditional Italian Dessert Made Simple

This recipe follows through the significant, while simple steps to achieve the perfect gelato, originating from the traditional Italian methods of Mokambo Gelateria. Whether you wish to create a wonderful gelato as a special treat for yourself, or as a performance to demonstrate an elaborate dessert, despite the lack of much effort, to family and friends. Otherwise, relax and enjoy as you read through a historically accurate representation of this beloved Italian dessert.

Prep Time

15 min

Cook Time

20 min

Serves

4–6

Ingredients

2 cups whole milk
1 cup heavy cream
3/4 cup sugar
5 egg yolks
1 tablespoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Heat milk and cream in a saucepan until steaming
  2. Whisk egg yolks with sugar until pale
  3. Slowly add hot milk to eggs while whisking
  4. Return mixture to saucepan and cook until thickened
  5. Strain and chill for at least 2 hours
  6. Churn in ice cream maker following manufacturer instructions
  7. Freeze for 4 hours before serving

Tips & Variations

  • Add crushed pistachios for a Sicilian twist.
  • Infuse the milk with lemon zest for a refreshing flavour.
  • Use real vanilla bean for a deeper aroma.
  • For chocolate gelato, add ½ cup melted dark chocolate.

About This Gelateria

Mokambo Gelateria is a traditional Italian ice cream shop located in Ruvo di Puglia. Their recipe dates back over 180 years, passed down through generations of skilled gelato makers. This gelateria is known for its authentic flavours, artisanal methods, and warm hospitality — making it a beloved destination for locals and visitors alike.
